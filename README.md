# The Multivac

Daily blind evaluation of frontier AI models.

## What This Is

One question. All major AI models. Anonymous evaluation. Every day.

The Multivac poses a single question to 10+ frontier AI models daily, evaluates their responses blindly, and publishes the results. Questions are fresh and varied — covering reasoning, creativity, technical depth, and philosophical inquiry — to prevent memorization and benchmark gaming.

## Methodology

- Models receive identical prompts with no system-level advantages
- Responses are evaluated before model identities are revealed
- No affiliations with any AI lab or company
- Full transparency in scoring criteria
